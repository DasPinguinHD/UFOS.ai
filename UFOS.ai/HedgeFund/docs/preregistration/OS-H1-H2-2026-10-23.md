## Hypotheses (verbatim)

- **H1 (market intraday momentum × gamma regime, after Baltussen et al. 2021).** Universe SPY (primary), QQQ
  (replication). r_first = return from the previous close to 10:00 New York; r_last = return 15:30 → 16:00. Regime at
  15:30 = sign of total GEX (baseline convention: calls +, puts −; OI with `available_at` ≤ decision time; chain
  snapshot at or before 15:25). Model: r_last = a + b·r_first + c·r_first·1[GEX<0] + e. **Prediction: c > 0**
  (momentum stronger in negative gamma). Trading rule for the evaluation: at 15:30 take a position in SPY shares in the
  direction of r_first only when GEX < 0, close at 15:59; costs as in the platform's cost model; benchmark = the same
  rule without the regime condition.
- **H2 (range in positive gamma).** The share of sessions whose close stays inside the morning's expected move is
  higher when GEX > 0 at 10:00 than when GEX < 0.
- **Evaluation:** forward data only (from the first archived day); first evaluation after ≥ 120 sessions;
  one-sided test at 5 %; robustness under all GEX variants (§6); the approximation backtest from 2024-02 is reported
  but labelled exploratory and does not count for the decision.
- **Stop criterion:** neither H1 nor H2 holds after ~6 months of archive + incubation → the structure layer stays an
  analysis tool, no structure-based trading.
