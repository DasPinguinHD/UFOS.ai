# Overfitting study of the UFOS validation gates (synthetic data)

MA-crossover family (256 configurations, UFOS grid of 64), Student-t (nu = 5) innovations iid or GARCH(1,1), planted signal Sharpe 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, training 5, 10, 20 years + 24-month holdout, best of K = 1, 8, 64, 256, costs 2 bps per unit turnover; M = 2000 paths per cell, seed = 20261109. risklab 0.5.0, numpy 2.5.3. No market data.

Gates in scope: G1 (time-series), G2, G4, G5, G6 (grid neighbours only), G7, H. Out of scope: G1 rank-IC form, G3, G6 costs and stress episodes, G8. Rates in %, ± Monte-Carlo se.
