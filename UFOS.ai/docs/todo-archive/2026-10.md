## Design ethos to keep in mind (MainWindow, 2026-09-21 brief)

The user explicitly asked to hold onto these while designing/implementing the
MainWindow:

- Use the global `Styles/DesignSystem.xaml` as much as possible for the
  MainWindow app shell. Compartmentalization (each module duplicating its own
  small style/config) should apply only to module-specific elements going
  forward, not to the shell itself. A unified design will also be required
  for future community modules.
- The MainWindow is allowed to feel a bit "nerdy" — keep the wink in mind that
  finance is often made to look more complicated than it is, on purpose, so
  outsiders don't realize how simple it actually is. That's the intended
  spirit behind the terminal/Bloomberg-key visual language.
- Don't change the design of the other windows (LiveStreamAgent, HedgeFund,
  etc.) as a side effect of this work — that's explicitly a separate,
  later task.
